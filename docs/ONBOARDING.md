# Onboarding Experience & User Flow

## Overview
The onboarding flow introduces players to Madhubani art traditions, cultural context, and touch gestures.

### Screens
1. **Welcome Screen**: Introduction to Mithila cultural art forms.
2. **Interactive Tools Preview**: Demonstrating brush, pencil, and flood fill.
3. **Canvas Orientation**: Pinch to zoom, double-tap reset, and pan interactions.
